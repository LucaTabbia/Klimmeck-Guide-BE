Ordini canonici (dai `@Prop` degli schemi, verificati):
- Lore: `type, image, name, description, relatedLore`
- Spell: `name, description, useType, energyDamage{type, power}, requiredLearnTime, minXpToLearn, recoveryTime, maxUsages`
- EquipmentItem: `name, itemType, description, sellPrice, buyPrice, rarity, equipType, addedSpell, damages{base[{type, power}], energy[{type, power}]}` (+ `sellable` in coda solo se presente nel file)
- LootItem: `name, itemType, description, sellPrice, buyPrice, rarity, power, effect` (+ `sellable` in coda solo se presente)
- Coins: `gold, silver, copper`
- Enemy: `name, locations, energyWeaknesses, baseWeaknesses, relatedLore, xp, spells, lifePoints, imagePath, damages`; ogni elemento di `spells`: `_id` (ObjectId della magia) + campi della Spell nell'ordine sopra
- Pet: `name, damages, buyPrice, sellPrice, xp, maxXp, currentLifePoints, maxLifePoints, imagePath, speedModifier`
- Quest `$set`: `infos{timeToComplete, title, enemy, type, markerLocation, relatedLore}`, `requirements{requiredPoints: null, requiredAdventurers: [], recommendedLoot[{itemType, item, quantity}], minTitle}`, `prizes{prizeCoins, xpPrize, prizeItem, randomLoot[…], spell, spellDraw}`; `$setOnInsert`: `{ registeredAdventurers: [], concludedAt: null, startedAt: null, rescue: null }`
- City update: solo `{ relatedLore: ObjectId }` (mai `area`, `pointsOfInterest`, `markerLocation`, `name`)
I campi solo-file (`id`, lore `city`, nemici `category`/`tier`/`source`, quest `city`) non entrano mai nei documenti.
