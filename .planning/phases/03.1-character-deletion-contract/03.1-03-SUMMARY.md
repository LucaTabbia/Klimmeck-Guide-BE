# Phase 03.1 Plan 03: Archived characters invisible on player paths Summary

**Archived characters now behave like non-existent ids on every HTTP player path. `CharacterAccessPolicy` checks existence with `exists(activeCharacterById(id))`, and `CharactersService` reads every Character through `findOne(activeCharacterById(id))` or `find(ACTIVE_CHARACTER_FILTER)`. The result is `NOT_FOUND` for the former owner, for other adventurers and for the innkeeper. The archived document is left untouched, and legacy documents without a `deletedAt` field stay visible.**
